- **fits**: fits that date and would be odd later;
- **either**: fits that date and later times too;
- **slip**: fits only a later time (an anachronism, or knowledge of a later event, office, text or controversy), or is wrong about its own time as independent evidence shows;
- **not**: cannot be checked against independent evidence.
