## 2. Every use

**Valentinus** (tier 1)

- Strom 6.6.52 ~ Ro 2:15: A, class A, hand: "the law written in the heart": γραπτός occurs in the NT only at Romans 2:15; Jeremiah 31:33 has the idea, not the word
- Strom 4.13.89 ~ Ro 8:21: D, class B, hand: "you rule over creation and all corruption": creation and corruption together, no shared phrase
- Strom 4.13.89 ~ 1Co 15:26: D, class A, hand: "that death may die": a common idea
- Strom 4.13.90 ~ Ro 1:20: D, class A, hand: "the invisible of God": common wording

**Basilides and Isidore** (tier 1)

- Strom 3.1.2 (Isidore) ~ Ga 5:4: D, class A, hand: "lest you be torn from the grace of God": weak
- Strom 3.1.3 (Isidore) ~ Ro 7:15: D, class A, hand: "does not do what he wants", in another sense
- Strom 2.20.113 (Isidore) ~ Ro 7:19: D, class A, hand: the excuse "I acted without wanting to", which Isidore rejects

**Ptolemy** (tier 1)

- Pan 33.5.15 ~ 1Co 5:7: Q, class A, candidate: "Paul the apostle": "our Passover was sacrificed, Christ" and "that you may be unleavened ... a new lump"
- Pan 33.6.6 ~ Eph 2:15: Q, class A, candidate: "the apostle Paul": "the law of commandments in decrees abolished"
- Pan 33.6.6 ~ Ro 7:12: Q, class A, candidate: "the law is holy, and the commandment holy, just and good"
- Pan 33.7.6 ~ 1Co 8:6: V, class A, candidate: "the Father, from whom are all things": the verse that is also among the strongest anti-Marcionite passages in Marcion's own edition
- Pan 33.5.11 ~ Ro 2:29: A, class A, hand: circumcision "of the heart, the spiritual one"
- Pan 33.5.10 ~ Ro 12:1: D, class A, hand: offerings "through spiritual praises": the idea is common

**Justin** (tier 1)

- Dial 39.1 ~ Ro 11:3-4: V, class B, candidate: Elijah's complaint in Paul's order and wording against the LXX: prophets killed before altars, "and I alone am left", "who have not bowed the knee to Baal" with the feminine article; could come from a shared collection of proof texts
- Dial 27.3 ~ Ro 3:11-17: V, class A, candidate: the chain of psalm and Isaiah verses in Paul's order; some LXX manuscripts of Psalm 13 carry the same chain, probably taken from Romans
- Dial 39.4; Dial 87.6 ~ Eph 4:8: V, class A, candidate: "he gave gifts to men", Paul's wording against the LXX "you received gifts among men"; the Targum also has "gave"
- Dial 95.1 ~ Ga 3:10: V, class A, candidate: Deuteronomy 27:26 in Paul's wording, not the LXX's ("all that is written in the book of the law to do them")
- Dial 96.1 ~ Ga 3:13: V, class A, candidate: "cursed is everyone who hangs on a tree", Paul's wording; the LXX has "cursed by God"
- Dial 78.11 ~ 1Co 1:19: A, class A, candidate: in a quotation of Isaiah 29:14, "I will set aside (ἀθετήσω)" as in Paul, against the LXX "I will hide"
- Dial 47.5 ~ Tit 3:4: D, class D, candidate: "the kindness and the love for mankind of God": four words in Titus' order, but the pairing is stock Hellenistic wording
- 1 Apol 52 ~ Ro 14:11: D, class A, candidate: "and every tongue shall confess", from Isaiah 45:23 in a form close to Paul's; Isaiah's text varies
- 2 Apol 5 ~ Col 1:16: D, class B, candidate: "all things through him": also John 1:3; common Logos language

## 4. Decision rule

A tier-1 witness uses a passage Marcion's edition lacked, graded Q or V: good evidence that a fuller Paul was in his rivals' hands by about 160.
- Justin Dial 39.1 ~ Ro 11:3-4 (V)

Tier-1 uses of the Pastorals (class D, Q to A): 0.

Named uses (Q) by groups outside Marcion's circle: Ptolemy (1Co, Eph, Ro); Basilidians reported by Clement (1Co); Theodotus (Eph, 1Co, Php, Ro).
