## 1. 2026-10-23: how a "puzzle explained" item is read (written after Part A ran, before Part B was coded)

PREDICTIONS.md section 5 asks whether one text explains something odd or unexplained in the other, but does not say
which way such an item points. The rule used, the usual one in source criticism: when a text keeps a detail without
its reason (a vestige), and the other text supplies the reason, the text with the vestige is coded as the later one,
since it kept the detail but dropped what explained it. A text that merely tells more is not coded as earlier or
later on that ground alone.
