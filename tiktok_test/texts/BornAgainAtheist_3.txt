Day 20 of Pride. Miss,
why is the L first in LGBTQIA+?
Well, it didn't always used to be.
The acronym used to be GLBT.
The switch was due to the lesbian activists
during the 1980s and 1990s Asian HIV crisis.
At a time where men in the community were heavily affected,
many lesbian activists provided advocacy,
caregiving, fundraising,
and support. Quite frankly,
when the world turned their back on these communities,
the lesbian Queens saved the day.
So to our lesbian heroes who volunteered as caregivers,
doctors who advocated, who helped in research, we say thank you.
