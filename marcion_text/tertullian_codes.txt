# passage | status | bearing | strength | attested | chapter | anchor | reason
#
# status:   in = Tertullian says or clearly implies it stood in Marcion's edition
#           changed = Tertullian says Marcion removed, added to or altered it
#           unclear
# bearing:  face = against Marcion's minimal theology on its face (PREDICTIONS.md 3.2)
#           argument = against Marcion only by Tertullian's argument
#           neutral = neutral or for Marcion
# strength: for face only: strong, medium, weak. Otherwise -
#           strong  = the letter's own words identify its God or Christ with the Creator or with Israel's scripture
#           medium  = Israel's scripture or law is cited with a formula ("it is written", "the law says") as authority
#                     for what the letter's God does or what Christians must do, or the law is called holy and good
#           weak    = the same without a citation formula, or the identification needs one inference
# attested: for status "in" only. explicit = Tertullian says Marcion kept it, "did not see" it, "did not erase" it, or
#           that Marcion or his followers read it a certain way; implicit = Tertullian argues from it as from
#           Marcion's text without saying so. Otherwise -
# chapter:  Against Marcion book 5 chapter, numbered as in the Perseus file (chapter 9 split off at Oehler's "Cap. IX.")
# anchor:   Latin words that must occur in that chapter (tertullian.py checks this)
