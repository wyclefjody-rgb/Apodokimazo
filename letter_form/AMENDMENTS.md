## 1. 2026-10-24: after the first run. Exploratory checks added; decision rules unchanged

The first run showed three detection problems. RESULTS.md section 6 reports checks on each; the pre-registered
measures and decision rules are left as they were.
- **False positives in the papyri.** All three papyri counted with a "grace benediction" use χάρις in its everyday
  sense ("thanks be to the gods", "the favour of Publius"). The true count of papyri with Paul's grace formulas is
  0 of 1,730, not 3. Section 6a lists them.
- **Long opening greetings.** Romans puts "grace to you and peace" at word 81, after a long prescript, so the
  60-word window misses it. Ignatius puts χαίρειν between words 21 and 92. Section 6b repeats both greeting
  searches over the first 150 words. With that window, Paul has grace and peace in 13 of 13 letters, and genuine
  Ignatius has the everyday χαίρειν in 6 of 7.
- **What drives the distances.** Section 6c splits the profile into length, the everyday frame (greeting and
  farewell) and the Christian formulas, because the fictional letters turned out to lack the everyday frame in
  these editions (prediction 4 assumed they would keep it).

## 2. 2026-10-24: which five formulas go into the profile

Section 3 of PREDICTIONS.md lists six formulas, but the profile uses "five". Section 4 names the profile's job
(everyday frame against Christian frame). The five used, fixed before the first run, are: everyday greeting,
grace and peace, everyday farewell, grace benediction and greetings passed on. The thanksgiving is left out,
because the papyri's health wish plays that role and is not measured.
