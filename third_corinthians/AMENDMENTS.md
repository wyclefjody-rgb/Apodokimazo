## 1. 2026-10-27: how the Latin texts were prepared (before Part B was run)

Source: Vouaux 1913, from the user's scan.
- **3 Corinthians:** printed pp. 248-252 (letter I) and 254-274 (letter III), scan pages 252-278. Vouaux prints a
  critical Latin text based mainly on the Laon manuscript (L2), with gaps filled from the other witnesses. The
  linking narrative (II) survives only in Coptic and Armenian, so it is used in Part A (in Vouaux's French) but not
  in Part B. Letters I and III are one text for Part B (647 words).
- **Paul to Seneca:** printed pp. 350-368, scan pages 354-372. Only Paul's six letters (II, IV, VI, VIII, X, XIV;
  452 words); Seneca's eight are left out.
- **Method:** the main text above the footnote rule was cropped and read by OCR (Latin), then corrected by eye
  against the page images:
  - footnote numbers and verse numbers removed;
  - æ and œ written ae and oe, as in the Vulgate file;
  - two first lines that the crop cut off restored from the images (3 Corinthians III.9-10 "cum Judaeos avellere
    vellet a delictis suis" and III.37-38 "acti erunt. Et quicumque taliter versantur, ii");
  - Vouaux's "..." in III.20 dropped;
  - in the dating lines of Paul to Seneca, Roman numerals dropped and "kal." written "kalendas".
- The text files are prepared from a modern edition: keep third_corinthians/texts/ out of public uploads, like
  rhetoric_across/laodiceans.txt.
