## What Tertullian says Marcion removed, added or altered

| passage | bearing of the canonical wording | chapter | what |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ga 2:3-5 | argument | 5.3 | Marcion's text reads "we did not yield even for an hour"; Tertullian calls this a corruption and reads "we yielded for a time". The dispute is about the negation, not about God |
| Ga 3:6-9 | face (strong) | 5.3 | Abraham believed God; scripture "preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham"; believers are Abraham's sons. Tertullian: removed, as the surviving "sons of faith" (3:26) shows |
| Ga 3:15b-16 | face (strong) | 5.4 | "no one annuls a man's covenant"; the promises were made "to Abraham and to his seed, which is Christ". Tertullian: Marcion's sponge removed it; his text joined "I speak in human terms" (3:15a) to 4:3 |
| Ga 4:22-26 | face (medium) | 5.4 | Abraham's two sons as an allegory of two covenants, the free woman "our mother". Kept, but Tertullian says Marcion partly altered it ("etsi ex parte convertit"), so counted as changed |
| 1Co 15:45 | face (medium) | 5.10 | "the last Adam" changed to "the last Lord" (Tertullian). The verse opens "thus it is written, The first man Adam became a living soul" and makes Christ the counterpart of the Creator's Adam |
| Ro 8:12-10:1 | face (strong) | 5.14 | Tertullian jumps from 8:11 to 10:2 over "a very wide gap of cut-out scripture". If the gap is the whole span it includes 9:4-5, Christ "from Israel according to the flesh"; its exact extent is not stated |
| Ro 10:5-11:32 | face (medium) | 5.14 | Tertullian: the scriptures and mysteries Paul had rehearsed before 11:33 were erased ("Qui tanta de scripturis ademisti"); the span is not stated |
| 1Th 2:14-16 | argument | 5.15 | "who killed the Lord and their own prophets": Tertullian says Marcion added "their own", separating the prophets from the Lord; the unaltered verse links them only by implication |
| 2Th 1:8 | argument | 5.16 | "in flaming fire" removed (Tertullian). That fire marks the Creator is Tertullian's argument |
| Eph 1:1 | neutral | 5.17 | the title: Marcion's edition called the letter "to the Laodiceans" |
| Eph 2:14 | argument | 5.17 | "in his flesh" changed to "in the flesh" (Tertullian): about Christ's flesh, not the minimal theology |
| Eph 2:20 | face (weak) | 5.17 | "the foundation of the apostles and prophets": "and prophets" removed. The prophets may be Christian prophets; Tertullian takes them as the old ones |
| Eph 3:8-10 | face (strong) | 5.18 | "hidden for ages in God who created all things" changed to "hidden for ages from the God who created all things" |
| Eph 6:1-3 | face (medium) | 5.18 | "this is the first commandment with a promise" removed; whether "Honour your father and mother" remained is not clear from Tertullian's words |
| Col 1:15b-16 | face (strong) | 5.19 | "the firstborn of all creation; in him all things were created, in heaven and on earth": Tertullian implies Marcion removed these words and kept only "he is before all" |
