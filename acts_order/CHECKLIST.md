# Part B: which telling answers the other (Acts-order study)

Rules: PREDICTIONS.md section 5 and AMENDMENTS.md 1 (a text that keeps a detail without its reason is coded as the
later one). Coded by Claude (an AI model), 2026-10-23, for review by a second reader. The Greek wording was checked
against the SBLGNT. Codes:
- **Acts answers the letters**: Acts looks like the later telling;
- **letters answer Acts**: the letters look like the later telling;
- **neither**: no direction can be read.

"Other reading" gives the best case for the opposite direction.
