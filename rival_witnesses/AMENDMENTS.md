## 1. 2026-10-27: the non-Greek witnesses are not coded yet

The rules call for coding the Epistle of the Apostles (tier 1), the Valentinian Nag Hammadi texts and Origen's
report of Basilides (tier 2) from published translations with scripture references. Those translations could not
be reached from the session in which this was run. They are left out of this run, and nothing in RESULTS.md counts
them. Adding them means adding lines to codes.txt (basis "translation").

Effect: prediction 1 is judged on four coded tier-1 witnesses out of five. If the Epistle of the Apostles has a Q
use (it names Paul in chapters 31-33), prediction 1 would be met. Its verdict is therefore provisional.

## 2. 2026-10-27: decisions made while setting up and coding, before the tally was run

- **Isidore** (Basilides' son and pupil) is coded with Basilides, as "Basilides and Isidore". His fragments are
  quoted word for word by Clement, like his father's. All their uses are graded D, so this changes no verdict.
- **Candidates that are not uses** are listed in not_uses.txt with a reason, so that every candidate has a code as
  section 4 requires. Most are stock phrases ("the Son of God", "our Lord Jesus Christ"). Several mentions of "the
  apostle" are not about Paul: Ptolemy calls John "the apostle" (John 1:3), and the Excerpts from Theodotus give
  "the apostle" for John 1:9 and 1:14, 1 Peter 1:19 and Luke 2:14. Justin's many mentions of "apostles" are of the
  Twelve, and he twice calls Jesus "apostle" (as Hebrews 3:1 does).
- **Source problems, handled by reading each candidate:**
  - The First1KGreek Hippolytus (opp-grc1) mixes the editor's notes into the text, including the Greek of Romans
    8:19 and 8:22. Every Hippolytus use was checked to be in Hippolytus' own text.
  - Clement's Excerpts from Theodotus mix Clement's comments with Valentinian material. Uses are coded where the
    wording is attributed to "the apostle", "Paul" or "they", or lies in the Valentinian sections; all are tier 2.
  - Ptolemy's Greek in the edition used has errors ("πάΠα" for πάσχα), so his quotation of 1 Corinthians 5:7 was
    found through the mention of "Paul the apostle", not through the 4-word search.
- **Justin's Second Apology** is cited by the Perseus numbering of the edition used.
- **One grading judgement to note:** Justin, Dialogue 47.5 shares four words in order with Titus 3:4 ("the
  kindness and the love for mankind"), which by itself meets the definition of V. It is graded D because that pair
  is stock Hellenistic wording, which section 4 lists as a reason for D. Graded V, it would make prediction 3 true
  for class D as well; no verdict changes.
