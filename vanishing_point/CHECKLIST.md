# Part B: datable passages, coded (Daniel-method study)

Rules: PREDICTIONS.md sections 3-4 and AMENDMENTS.md. Coded by Claude (an AI model), 2026-10-23, then reviewed
by a second reader (the user); where they differ the reviewer's call is the one counted and the first call is kept in
the "why" column (AMENDMENTS.md 3). Every call states its reason so a reader can disagree with it. Codes:
- **before 70**: fits the 30s-60s and would be an anachronism, an embarrassment or a pointless invention in 144
- **after 70** / **after 135**: fits only a later time (a slip, in Daniel's sense)
- **either**: fits both, or a later writer could easily have written it as setting
Strength: strong / medium / weak. "Livesey-side reply" gives the best answer a supporter of a 144 date could make.
