3 Corinthians | I.4-5; III.4 | the teaching received from the other apostles | slip | medium | the Corinthians have heard "from you and from the other apostles", and Paul says he handed on what he "received from the holy apostles before me, who were with the Lord Jesus Christ all the time"; this contradicts Paul's own claim that he received his gospel from no human being (Galatians 1:1, 1:11-12, 1:17); Vouaux calls it an anachronism showing the 2nd century's concern with apostolic tradition
